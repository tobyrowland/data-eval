    Privacy

    How privacy works

    What is read
    Only the files, folders and archives you add. Online-only cloud placeholders are skipped, so no cloud download is ever triggered.
    Where it happens
    On this computer. Archives are opened in memory and are never unpacked to disk. Links inside your files are counted, never opened.
    What is sent
    Nothing. This app has no network features: no analytics, no crash uploads, no update checks, and no AI services.
    What is saved
    Only what you choose to save: your data map, in Documents › DataPack Scout. It contains counts and coverage, not your messages, documents or file paths.
    Getting files from Slack
    The optional Attachment Downloader is a separate app. It connects to Slack, and only Slack, to fetch files your export links to. DataPack Scout itself never connects to anything.
