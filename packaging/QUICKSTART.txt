Working Minds 0.1.0 (preview)
==============================

Understand your company data, privately. Assessment happens on this
computer. No data is sent to us or to an AI service.

HOW TO USE
1. Double-click "working-minds" (Windows: working-minds.exe).
   A window opens.
2. Drag an export (a ZIP, a folder or a file) into the window and press Enter.
   Repeat for as many exports as you like.
3. Press Enter on an empty line to start.
4. Your data map appears in the window and is saved in
   Documents/Working Minds as Your-Data-Map-<date>.txt and .json.

FIRST-RUN WARNINGS (this preview is not yet code-signed)
- Windows: if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click
  "More info", then "Run anyway".
- macOS: if it says the app "cannot be opened", right-click (or
  Control-click) working-minds, choose Open, then Open again.
  Or run this in Terminal from the unzipped folder:
      xattr -d com.apple.quarantine working-minds

ADVANCED (command line)
  working-minds scan <file-or-folder>... [--exclude <path>] [--json out.json]

WHAT THIS PREVIEW DOES
It inventories files, folders and archives (ZIP, TAR, GZIP). It reads CSV,
JSON, text, email and Slack exports, and reports what it found. It also
reports how much it could examine, and what is missing or locked. PDF/Office
text extraction, the PDF report and the encrypted assessment come in later
releases.
